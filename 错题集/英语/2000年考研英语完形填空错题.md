## 题目

**2000 年全国硕士研究生招生考试 · 统考英语 Section I Use of English（10 分）**

```
If a farmer wishes to succeed, he must try to keep a wide gap between his consumption and his production. He must store a large quantity of grain  __1__  consuming all his grain immediately. He can continue to support himself and his family  __2__  he produces a surplus. He must use this surplus in three ways: as seed for sowing, as an insurance  __3__  the unpredictable effects of bad weather and as a commodity which he must sell in order to  __4__  old agricultural implements and obtain chemical fertilizers to  __5__  the soil. He may also need money to construct irrigation  __6__, and improve his farm in other ways. If no surplus is available, a farmer cannot be  __7__. He must either sell some of his property or  __8__  extra funds in the form of loans. Naturally he will try to borrow money at a low  __9__  of interest, but loans of this kind are not  __10__  obtainable.
```

## 根因分析

未作答。完形填空失分集中在三类：①**介词固定搭配**（insurance against、rate of interest）；②**名词与动词近义辨析**（channels 是 irrigation 的固定搭配，replace 表「以新换旧」）；③**逻辑衔接词**（instead of 表取舍对比，only if 表唯一条件）。需要建立「先看搭配、再看逻辑、最后辨词义」的解题顺序。

## 完整知识点梳理

### 高频固定搭配

| 搭配 | 含义 | 本题出处 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| insurance **against** sth. | 防范…的保障 | 第 3 题 |
| irrigation **channels** | 灌溉渠道 | 第 6 题 |
| **rate** of interest | 利率 | 第 9 题 |
| seek funds / seek help | 寻求资金/帮助 | 第 8 题 |
| feed the soil | 养地、肥田 | 第 5 题 |

### 近义词辨析

| 词组 | 区别 |
| --- | --- |
| instead of / other than / more than | instead of「而不是」（取舍）；other than「除了」；more than「多于、不仅仅」 |
| only if / ever since | only if「只有…才」（必要条件）；ever since「自从…以来」 |
| replace / purchase / supplement / dispose | replace「更换旧物」；purchase「购买」；supplement「补充」；dispose「处理掉」 |
| proportion / percentage / rate / ratio | rate「率」（rate of interest 利率）；ratio「比值」；percentage「百分比」；proportion「比例、部分」 |
| self-sufficient / self-confident | self-sufficient「自给自足」；self-confident「自信」；self-satisfied「自满」（贬义）；self-restrained「自制」 |

### 逻辑解题要点

1. 第 1 空：store grain 与 consuming immediately 是二选一关系 → instead of。
2. 第 2 空：支持家庭的前提是产出盈余 → only if（必要条件）。
3. 第 7 空：没有盈余就无法「自给自足」→ 语义决定 self-sufficient。
4. 第 10 空：这类贷款「不经常」可获得 → frequently 修饰 obtainable。

**记忆口诀**：

> **「搭配先行、逻辑定案」**——insurance against / rate of interest / irrigation channels 见到直接锁定；先判断空格前后是取舍、条件还是频率，再选词。

## 复刷清单

### 举一反三 5 题

1. 【单选】As an insurance ______ unexpected losses, farmers usually store some grain.（A. for　B. against　C. of　D. towards）**答案：B**。insurance against… 固定搭配。
2. 【单选】He will succeed ______ he keeps a wide gap between consumption and production.（A. only if　B. even if　C. as if　D. if only）**答案：A**。only if「只有…才」，必要条件。
3. 【单选】They borrowed money at a low ______ of interest.（A. proportion　B. percentage　C. rate　D. ratio）**答案：C**。rate of interest「利率」。
4. 【单选】The government invested heavily in irrigation ______.（A. vessels　B. routes　C. paths　D. channels）**答案：D**。irrigation channels「灌溉渠道」。
5. 【单选】He sold the surplus to ______ old implements with new machines.（A. replace　B. purchase　C. supplement　D. dispose）**答案：A**。replace…with…「以新换旧」。

### 历年真题风格练习

1. 【单选】Farmers must store grain ______ consuming it immediately.（A. other than　B. as well as　C. instead of　D. more than）**答案：C**。表取舍「而不是」。
2. 【单选】Without a surplus, a farmer cannot be ______.（A. self-confident　B. self-sufficient　C. self-satisfied　D. self-restrained）**答案：B**。语义「自给自足」。
3. 【单选】He must sell part of his property or ______ extra funds in the form of loans.（A. search　B. save　C. offer　D. seek）**答案：D**。seek funds「寻求资金」；search 需接 for。
4. 【单选】Chemical fertilizers are used to ______ the soil.（A. enhance　B. mix　C. feed　D. raise）**答案：C**。feed the soil「肥田」。
5. 【单选】Loans of this kind are not ______ obtainable.（A. genuinely　B. obviously　C. presumably　D. frequently）**答案：D**。副词修饰 obtainable 表频率。
