## 原文（Text 1，2000 年考研英语阅读理解）

### 第一段

① A history of long and effortless success can be a dreadful handicap, but, if properly handled, it may become a driving force. ② When the United States entered just such a glowing period after the end of the Second World War, it had a market eight times larger than any competitor, giving its industries unparalleled economies of scale. ③ Its scientists were the world’s best; its workers the most skilled. ④ America and Americans were prosperous beyond the dreams of the Europeans and Asians whose economies the war had destroyed.

### 第二段

① It was inevitable that this primacy should have narrowed as other countries grew richer. ② Just as inevitably, the retreat from predominance proved painful. ③ By the mid-1980s Americans had found themselves at a loss over their fading industrial competitiveness. ④ Some huge American industries, such as consumer electronics, had shrunk or vanished in the face of foreign competition. ⑤ By 1987 there was only one American television maker left, Zenith. (Now there is none: Zenith was bought by South Korea’s LG Electronics in July.) ⑥ Foreign-made cars and textiles were sweeping into the domestic market. ⑦ America’s machine-tool industry was on the ropes. ⑧ For a while it looked as though the making of semiconductors, which America had invented and which sat at the heart of the new computer age, was going to be the next casualty.

### 第三段

① All of this caused a crisis of confidence. ② Americans stopped taking prosperity for granted. ③ They began to believe that their way of doing business was failing, and that their incomes would therefore shortly begin to fall as well. ④ The mid-1980s brought one inquiry after another into the causes of America’s industrial decline. ⑤ Their sometimes sensational findings were filled with warnings about the growing competition from overseas.

### 第四段

① How things have changed! ② In 1995 the United States can look back on five years of solid growth while Japan has been struggling. ③ Few Americans attribute this solely to such obvious causes as a devalued dollar or the turning of the business cycle. ④ Self-doubt has yielded to blind pride. ⑤ “American industry has changed its structure, has gone on a diet, has learnt to be more quick-witted,” according to Richard Cavanagh, executive dean of Harvard’s Kennedy School of Government. ⑥ “It makes me proud to be an American just to see how our businesses are improving their productivity,” says Stephen Moore of the Cato Institute, a think-tank in Washington, DC. ⑦ And William Sahlman of the Harvard Business School believes that people will look back on this period as “a golden age of business management in the United States.”

## 全文逐句翻译（中英对照）

### 第一段逐句翻译

1. **A history of long and effortless success can be a dreadful handicap, but, if properly handled, it may become a driving force.**  
   长期轻而易得的成功史可能成为可怕的障碍，但如果处理得当，它也可能成为推动力。

2. **When the United States entered just such a glowing period after the end of the Second World War, it had a market eight times larger than any competitor, giving its industries unparalleled economies of scale.**  
   二战结束后，美国恰好进入这样一段辉煌时期：它拥有比任何竞争者大八倍的市场，使本国产业获得无与伦比的规模经济。

3. **Its scientists were the world’s best; its workers the most skilled.**  
   它的科学家是世界最优秀的，它的工人是技术最熟练的。

4. **America and Americans were prosperous beyond the dreams of the Europeans and Asians whose economies the war had destroyed.**  
   美国和美国人富足到超出那些经济被战争摧毁的欧洲人与亚洲人的想象。

### 第二段逐句翻译

1. **It was inevitable that this primacy should have narrowed as other countries grew richer.**  
   随着其他国家日益富裕，美国的这种首要地位缩小是不可避免的。

2. **Just as inevitably, the retreat from predominance proved painful.**  
   同样不可避免的是，从主导地位后退被证明是痛苦的。

3. **By the mid-1980s Americans had found themselves at a loss over their fading industrial competitiveness.**  
   到20世纪80年代中期，美国人发现自己面对日渐衰弱的产业竞争力不知所措。

4. **Some huge American industries, such as consumer electronics, had shrunk or vanished in the face of foreign competition.**  
   面对外国竞争，美国一些大型产业（如消费电子业）已经萎缩甚至消失。

5. **By 1987 there was only one American television maker left, Zenith. (Now there is none: Zenith was bought by South Korea’s LG Electronics in July.)**  
   到1987年，美国只剩一家电视制造商——Zenith。（如今一家也没有了：Zenith 已于7月被韩国 LG 电子收购。）

6. **Foreign-made cars and textiles were sweeping into the domestic market.**  
   外国制造的汽车和纺织品正在大举涌入国内市场。

7. **America’s machine-tool industry was on the ropes.**  
   美国的机床制造业岌岌可危。

8. **For a while it looked as though the making of semiconductors, which America had invented and which sat at the heart of the new computer age, was going to be the next casualty.**  
   有那么一段时间，半导体制造似乎将成为下一个“牺牲品”——半导体是美国人发明的，并处于新计算机时代的核心。

### 第三段逐句翻译

1. **All of this caused a crisis of confidence.**  
   所有这一切引发了一场信心危机。

2. **Americans stopped taking prosperity for granted.**  
   美国人不再把繁荣视为理所当然。

3. **They began to believe that their way of doing business was failing, and that their incomes would therefore shortly begin to fall as well.**  
   他们开始相信，自己的经营方式正在失败，因此收入不久也会下降。

4. **The mid-1980s brought one inquiry after another into the causes of America’s industrial decline.**  
   20世纪80年代中期，针对美国产业衰退原因的调查一个接一个。

5. **Their sometimes sensational findings were filled with warnings about the growing competition from overseas.**  
   这些调查有时耸人听闻的结论，充满了对海外竞争日益加剧的警告。

### 第四段逐句翻译

1. **How things have changed!**  
   情况变化真大啊！

2. **In 1995 the United States can look back on five years of solid growth while Japan has been struggling.**  
   1995年，美国可以回顾五年的稳定增长，而日本却一直在挣扎。

3. **Few Americans attribute this solely to such obvious causes as a devalued dollar or the turning of the business cycle.**  
   几乎没有美国人把这仅仅归因于美元贬值或经济周期转折这类显而易见的原因。

4. **Self-doubt has yielded to blind pride.**  
   自我怀疑已经让位于盲目自豪。

5. **“American industry has changed its structure, has gone on a diet, has learnt to be more quick-witted,” according to Richard Cavanagh, executive dean of Harvard’s Kennedy School of Government.**  
   哈佛大学肯尼迪政府学院执行院长 Richard Cavanagh 说：“美国工业已经改变了结构，进行了精简，也学会了更加机敏。”

6. **“It makes me proud to be an American just to see how our businesses are improving their productivity,” says Stephen Moore of the Cato Institute, a think-tank in Washington, DC.**  
   华盛顿智库卡托研究所的 Stephen Moore 说：“只要看到我们的企业正在提高生产率，我就为自己是美国人而自豪。”

7. **And William Sahlman of the Harvard Business School believes that people will look back on this period as “a golden age of business management in the United States.”**  
   哈佛商学院的 William Sahlman 认为，人们日后回顾这一时期时，会把它视为“美国企业管理的黄金时代”。

## 逐题解析

### 第 11 题（已答对）

**The U.S. achieved its predominance after World War II because _____.**  
答案 C：the war had destroyed the economies of most potential competitors。第一段④句说美国人的繁荣超出“经济被战争摧毁的欧亚人”的想象，表明战后优势的重要背景是潜在竞争者经济被战争破坏。A“付出艰苦努力”与 effortless 相反；B 把“比竞争者大八倍”偷换成“比以前大八倍”；D 把“市场大/规模经济”偷换成“劳动力规模”。

### 第 12 题（错题：细节理解）

**The loss of U.S. predominance in the world economy in the 1980s is manifested in the fact that the American _____.**  
正确答案 D：auto industry had lost part of its domestic market。第二段⑥句“Foreign-made cars and textiles were sweeping into the domestic market”直接说明外国汽车与纺织品涌入美国国内市场，即美国汽车业失去部分国内市场。  
**用户答案 A 错因**：A 说电视业“撤回到国内市场”，但原文只说 1987 年只剩 Zenith 一家电视制造商，后来被韩国 LG 收购；既没有“撤回”，也不是“衰退表现”的正确同义改写。B“半导体被外资接管”、C“机床业因自杀性行为崩溃”均为无中生有/曲解 on the ropes。

### 第 13 题（错题：推理判断）

**What can be inferred from the passage?**  
正确答案 B：Intense competition may contribute to economic progress。文章主线是：战后竞争弱时美国轻松取得优势 → 他国变富后优势缩小、外国竞争冲击产业并引发信心危机 → 美国反思调整后走向复苏。这说明竞争压力可能间接促进经济进步。  
**用户答案 A 错因**：A 把第四段“Self-doubt has yielded to blind pride”这一特定时期美国人的心理变化扩大为“人类本性”，属于典型的范围夸大；C“国际合作”无中生有；D 与首段“长期轻松成功可能是障碍”相反。

### 第 14 题（错题：隐含原因与作者态度）

**The author seems to believe the revival of the U.S. economy in the 1990s can be attributed to the _____.**  
正确答案 A：turning of the business cycle。第四段③句说“几乎没有美国人把这仅仅归因于美元贬值或经济周期转折这类显而易见的原因”，④句紧接着说“自我怀疑让位于盲目自豪”。作者借 Few 和 blind pride 暗示：美元贬值和经济周期这些明显原因被美国人的自豪情绪忽视了。  
**用户答案 B 错因**：B“产业结构重组”来自 Cavanagh 的话，C“管理改善”来自 Moore 和 Sahlman 的话；这些都是被引者的解释，且出现在“blind pride”的语境中，不代表作者本人结论。D“教育成功”未提及。

## 完整知识点梳理

### 一、细节理解：原文同义改写

- 题干关键词：By the mid-1980s、1987、domestic market、machine-tool、semiconductors。
- 原文证据链：shrink/vanish → sweeping into → on the ropes → next casualty。
- 同义改写：foreign-made cars … sweeping into domestic market = auto industry lost part of domestic market。

### 二、推理判断：小步推出，不扩大

- 首段：long and effortless success → dreadful handicap（如果处理不当）。
- 第二段：其他国变富 → primacy narrowed → retreat painful。
- 第三段：crisis of confidence → inquiry → warnings about overseas competition。
- 第四段：solid growth → self-doubt yielded to blind pride → 专家乐观引语。
- 合理推论：竞争压力可能促使经济反思与进步，而不是“人性如此”或“合作依赖”。

### 三、隐含原因与作者态度

- Few Americans attribute this solely to such obvious causes …：Few 构成否定框架，solely 暗示“不止这些”但美国人没认识到。
- Self-doubt has yielded to blind pride：blind 是明确贬义评价词。
- 专家引语在 blind pride 之后：作者是在展示美国人的盲目自豪，而不是背书“管理黄金时代”。

### 四、文章结构速览

```mermaid
flowchart LR
A[战后优势：市场大、科学家与工人优秀] --> B[他国变富：优势缩小]
B --> C[1980年代：产业萎缩、外国竞争、信心危机]
C --> D[调查与警告：海外竞争加剧]
D --> E[1990年代：稳定增长、盲目自豪]
E --> F[作者暗示：明显原因被忽视，竞争促反思]
```

## 考研速记规律（一看就会）

1. **三对齐秒杀细节题**：选项中的主体、时间、范围与原文不一致就排除；1987 只剩 Zenith ≠ 电视业撤回国内；韩国 LG ≠ 日本企业。
2. **负面词链定位衰退**：fading、shrunk、vanished、sweeping into、on the ropes、casualty 串联成“衰退链”；看到这些词，选项中出现 solid growth/golden age 必属跨段错位。
3. **推理题一小步原则**：原文说“某人/某时期自信膨胀”，只能推“这时期心态变化”，不能推“human nature”；正确答案常是 may/can 这类克制表达。
4. **Few/solely 是高信号词**：Few = 几乎没有（否定）；solely = 仅仅；二者同句出现，作者往往在暗示“被忽视的原因”，不要被后面专家引语带走。
5. **引语态度看上下文**：引语前是 blind pride → 引语是反讽/例证；引语前是 evidence shows → 引语可能是论据。专家说 ≠ 作者说。
6. **阅读题型定位口诀**：细节回原文，推理一小步，词汇看语境，态度抓评价，主旨串首尾。

   A. Foreign-made cars and textiles were sweeping into the domestic market.　B. American scientists became less skilled than before.　C. Japan bought the only remaining U.S. television maker.　D. The United States invented semiconductors.
   【中文】根据第二段，哪一发展最直接表明美国产业竞争力下降？
   **答案：A**。第二段⑥句直接说外国汽车和纺织品涌入国内市场，是美国失去部分国内市场的具体表现。B 与第一段“世界最好”矛盾；C 中买方是韩国 LG 且顺序错；D 是美国的优势事实，不是衰退信号。

2. 【词义猜测-熟词僻义】In “America’s machine-tool industry was on the ropes,” the phrase “on the ropes” most nearly means _____.
   A. expanding rapidly　B. in serious trouble　C. under foreign ownership　D. protected by tariffs
   【中文】“美国机床业 on the ropes”中该短语最接近什么意思？
   **答案：B**。前文列举消费电子萎缩、电视制造商只剩一家，语境表明机床业同样处境危险；on the ropes 是拳击用语，引申为“岌岌可危”。

3. 【细节理解-比对选项】Which statement about the U.S. TV industry is supported by the passage?
   A. It had withdrawn to its domestic market by 1987.　B. Only one U.S. maker remained by 1987, and it was later bought by a Korean company.　C. It was taken over by Japanese enterprises in July.　D. It lost part of its domestic market to textiles.
   【中文】关于美国电视业，哪一项得到文章支持？
   **答案：B**。第二段⑤句说 1987 年只剩 Zenith 一家，括号内补充它后来被韩国 LG 收购；A 原文未说“撤回到国内市场”，C 国别错误，D 张冠李戴。

4. 【细节理解-例证目的】Why does the author mention semiconductors in paragraph 2?
   A. To show that U.S. decline had already affected every industry.　B. To give an example that even a U.S.-invented core industry seemed likely to become the next casualty.　C. To prove that the United States had lost all technological advantages.　D. To introduce the history of the computer market.
   【中文】第二段为什么提到半导体？
   **答案：B**。半导体是美国发明且处于新计算机时代核心，但连它都“似乎要成为下一个牺牲品”，用来强化衰退之痛；A、C 过度绝对，D 非写作目的。

5. 【细节理解-定位】Which detail best illustrates “the retreat from predominance proved painful”?
   A. The U.S. market was eight times larger than any competitor’s.　B. European and Asian economies had been destroyed by the war.　C. Consumer-electronics industries shrank or vanished under foreign competition.　D. Experts praised the golden age of business management.
   【中文】哪一细节最能说明“优势后退是痛苦的”？
   **答案：C**。“主导地位后退的痛苦”紧接着由消费电子萎缩/消失、外国商品涌入、机床业岌岌可危等细节说明；A、B 属战后优势背景，D 属 1990 年代复苏。

### 历年真题风格练习

1. 【细节理解-例证识别】The author uses Zenith as an example to show _____.
   A. the strength of American television technology　B. the shrinking or vanishing of huge American industries under foreign competition　C. the success of South Korean companies in the U.S. market　D. the turning of the business cycle
   【中文】作者以 Zenith 为例是为了说明_____。
   **答案：B**。Zenith 的例子紧跟“Some huge American industries … had shrunk or vanished”，是外国竞争下美国产业萎缩/消失的例证；C 只是括号中的附带信息，不是例证目的。

2. 【细节理解-句意转换】By “Foreign-made cars and textiles were sweeping into the domestic market,” the author means _____.
   A. American cars and textiles were exported in large quantities　B. foreign products rapidly gained share in the U.S. market　C. the domestic market was closed to foreign goods　D. the U.S. auto industry improved its productivity
   【中文】“外国汽车和纺织品涌入国内市场”意为_____。
   **答案：B**。sweep into 表示“大举涌入”，domestic market 指国内市场，因此句意是外国产品迅速进入并占据美国市场份额。

3. 【细节理解-排除题】Which of the following is NOT mentioned as a sign of U.S. industrial decline?
   A. Consumer electronics shrank or vanished.　B. The machine-tool industry was on the ropes.　C. Semiconductor making seemed to be the next casualty.　D. The education system collapsed.
   【中文】以下哪项未被提及为美国产业衰退的迹象？
   **答案：D**。A、B、C 均在第二段出现；教育系统崩溃文中未提及，属于无中生有。
